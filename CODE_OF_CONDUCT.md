# Community code of conduct

Education Agent Skills is intended to be a rigorous, welcoming public resource for educators, researchers, learners, and builders from different contexts.

## Expected conduct

When participating in issues, discussions, reviews, or contributions:

- engage with people and ideas respectfully;
- explain disagreements with evidence and concrete reasoning;
- distinguish research findings, practitioner judgement, and personal preference;
- remain open about uncertainty, limitations, and contextual differences;
- protect student, educator, and community privacy;
- make space for contributors with different levels of technical and educational experience;
- accept constructive correction and repair harm when it occurs.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualised conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publication of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Neither is using educational status, technical expertise, or research credentials to demean or silence others.

## Educational privacy

Do not publish identifiable student records, private school documents, confidential assessment data, credentials, access tokens, or material you do not have permission to share. Use fictional or safely anonymised examples when demonstrating a problem.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct or privacy concerns privately to `gareth.manning@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Anyone handling a report should protect the reporter's privacy and avoid conflicts of interest. This code applies in repository spaces and in public interactions where someone is formally representing the project.
